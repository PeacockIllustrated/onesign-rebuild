# Brand briefs, condensed

## OneLaser
Positioning: the fabrication floor's precision offered as a service. From the team behind Onesign & Digital. Audience: engineers, fabricators, contractors, OEM buyers who judge you by tolerances and turnaround.
Voice: clipped, technical, confident. Numbers do the persuading. Approved headline: "cut clean, every pass."
Landing structure (as reference): dark beam hero with readout row → capabilities strip (contained to wrap, first cell flush left) → process 01-04 → materials with data tables → DXF upload band (centrepiece CTA) → recent cuts → footer with group cross-link.
Case studies are evidence of tolerance held, not galleries. Job number, spec plate, parameters, measured variance, client verdict, proof stats. Flagship: Specialty Services, structured as the journey to the piece in situ (see docs/v2-interactions.md).
Phase 2: instant-quote DXF upload tool (highest-leverage software differentiator; separate scoped build).

## OneDesign
Positioning: a studio with a factory attached. Brands designed to survive the real world, bolted to buildings, cut from steel, lit at night. Audience: owners and marketers commissioning identity and environment work.
Voice: warm, concept-first, plain-spoken. Approved headline: "ideas that take shape."
Landing structure (as reference): dark ASCII-mark hero → manifesto (full container width) → three services (hover to solid teal) → asymmetric work grid seeded with real projects (Community Shop Sunderland, Dirty Murphy's, The Tanning Co) → process ribbon ending on "make it real" → studio-with-a-factory section → big centred CTA → footer.
Case studies are stories: challenge, idea, system reveal with per-project palette, "then we walked it downstairs" cross-brand strip. Flagship: Tanglewood Equestrian (brochure, leaflets, signage set, website, all design work). Scroll-adaptive brand takeover and ASCII icon heroes specified in docs/v2-interactions.md.
